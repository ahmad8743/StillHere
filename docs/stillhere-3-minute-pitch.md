# STILL HERE: Hack Midwest Video Pitch

**Target runtime: 2 minutes 55 seconds**

**0:00–0:25 | Opening (Raneem)**

*Screen: STILL HERE homepage, with your camera overlay in the corner.*

"Imagine your child asking, 'Where are we sleeping tonight?'

You've just lost your home to a hurricane. You have $300 left, no ID, and no idea where to turn.

Help might be available. But can you get there? Do you qualify? And will your money last until that help arrives?

That's why we built STILL HERE."

**0:25–1:15 | Recovery demo (Raneem)**

*Screen: Select affected by disaster → safe for now → shelter and food → missing ID and transportation.*

"STILL HERE is an AI-powered recovery companion that helps people figure out their next steps after a disaster.

Let's say I'm in Louisiana after a hurricane.

First, the app checks whether I'm safe. Then it asks what I need. I can select food and somewhere to stay, because in real life, emergencies don't happen one at a time.

But here's what makes STILL HERE different.

Finding a shelter online is one thing. Actually getting there without transportation or an ID is another.

Our app considers those obstacles and provides guidance based on my situation. And when I come back, my recovery plan is still there."

**1:15–2:10 | Flex FinTech demonstration (Raneem)**

*Screen: Open budget → $300 starting balance → no support → aid day 8 → aid day 12.*

"Now let's talk about money, because finding help is only half the problem.

We built a financial planning tool that shows how long your available money could last while you're waiting for assistance.

Here, I have $300. Based on these sample expenses, I run out of money on day five.

Now suppose food and lodging are covered for a week, and I receive $500 in assistance on day eight. Suddenly, my expenses are covered for the next two weeks.

But watch what happens when that exact same payment arrives on day twelve.

*Pause briefly on the shortfall.*

I'm now short on day eleven.

Same amount of help. Just four days later. A completely different outcome.

Because sometimes, getting help isn't enough. **Getting it on time is what matters.**"
